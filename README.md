## 📝 How I want to document my learning

For future lab write-ups, my goal is to explain:

1. **Context:** The authorized training environment and learning objective.
2. **Finding:** What happened and why it matters.
3. **Cause:** The underlying application behavior.
4. **Fix:** How the issue can be prevented or mitigated.
5. **Reflection:** What I learned and what to explore next.
